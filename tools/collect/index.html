Help teach the pen.

You'll write 18 short words, one at a time, with your finger, a stylus or a mouse. After each word we'll show which strokes we think make each letter. Fix anything that's wrong, then move on. It takes about five minutes.

Before you start

Your handwriting will be used to train an open-source model and may be published with it.

Is every letter the right colour?

Each colour is one letter. Tap a letter below, then tap the strokes that belong to it. If one stroke runs into the next letter, turn on Cut and tap where it should split. Drag a cut to move it; tap it again to remove it.
